# Harshit Kunwar

I build products that make complex software easier to use.

Most of my work sits somewhere between interface understanding, workflow automation, and products for people who shouldn't need to be deeply technical just to get real work done.

Sometimes that turns into browser-native operator tooling. Sometimes it turns into small consumer products with a clear daily use case. The pattern is the same: reduce friction, keep the workflow honest, and ship something real.

## What Ties It Together

- software should adapt to people, not just the other way around
- repetitive workflow work should be automatable
- non-technical users deserve better operating surfaces
- AI is most useful when it can see state, act carefully, and leave a trail

Some of the messier implementation-heavy systems stay private until they’re clean enough to show. The public repos are the parts that best reflect how I think and build.
